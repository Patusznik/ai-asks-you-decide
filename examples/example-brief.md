# RoastLog · Product Brief
_Example brief from a sample session. Fictional user: a backend dev who roasts coffee at home._

## One-line promise
RoastLog: link each roast to how it tasted so you can repeat the good ones.

## Who it's for
A beginner home roaster in their first year, roasting in a popcorn popper or a small machine, who keeps notes on paper or in Excel and can't repeat a good roast.

## Core loop
Start roast → tap "first crack" → end roast → reminder 3 days later → rate the taste next to the roast notes

## Signature mechanic
The tasting reminder that links back to the roast.

## Decision log
1. **One promise: repeat the good roasts**: because the pain is not knowing what I did differently, because the roast and the tasting are days apart, because I lost my best Ethiopia and never made it again.
2. **The reminder is the heart, not AI**: because without the link it's just Excel.
3. **Bluetooth thermometer stays in v1**: because I think people won't log temperatures by hand.

## Not in v1
- AI roast profile suggestions
- "Repeat roast" button
- Flutter vs React Native debate (pick one and go)
- Domain hunting
- Dashboard with charts
